InviteIZI original instrumental recordings and scores

The scores and the master recordings in this directory's manifest were created specifically for InviteIZI by independent additive synthesis. No third-party melodies, sound recordings, samples, performances or soundfonts were imported. The source renderer and the complete motifs, harmony rules, tempo and instrument parameters are included in scripts/render-original-music.mjs and manifest.json.
